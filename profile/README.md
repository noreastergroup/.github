# Noreaster Group

**We build digital products for your business.**

We're a development agency that partners with businesses to deliver custom web applications, iOS apps, and Android apps. Our tech stack is built for lasting power and easy maintenance, so your product grows with your business.

## What We Build

- **Web Applications** — Full-stack apps built with Ruby on Rails, Hotwire, and modern frameworks that scale with your business
- **Mobile Apps** — Hotwire Native on iOS and Android delivers exceptional user experiences with the power of Rails
- **API Development** — Robust, scalable APIs that power your digital ecosystem and integrate with third-party services

## Our Stack

- Ruby on Rails
- Hotwire & Turbo
- Hotwire Native (iOS & Android)
- PostgreSQL
- Tailwind CSS
